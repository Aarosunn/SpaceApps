## 2. REQUIRED SUBMISSION FIELDS (2025 guide, recheck in 2026)
Source: Paris kit rules.md [VERIFIED 3rd party, labelled "(2025)"], drawn from project-submission-guide.
1. Project name
2. High-level summary
3. Demo link (project demo)
4. Final project link
5. Detailed description (what it does, how it works, benefits, goals, tools/languages)
6. NASA data used, and how
7. Space agency partner and other data/resources (credit ALL code, text, images = references)
8. Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI)
9. Submission verification (accept terms, originality)
- Every field must be complete and in English to be eligible for Global Judging [VERIFIED 3rd party; English rule also SNIPPET from the official guide].
- References: the judging guide says to confirm originality and to list ALL external resources (code, text, images), "even if open source", in a References field. Under the 2026 Participant Terms, NASA may remove materials that do not cite references properly - judging-awards-guide + https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/legal/ [SNIPPET].
- Length limits: [UNKNOWN] (not found in any snippet).
- The project must be submitted through the team's project page on spaceappschallenge.org - judging-awards-guide [SNIPPET].

## 3. DEMO RULES
- "Video (30 seconds or less) or slide deck (up to seven slides including title and intro slides)" - project-submission-guide [SNIPPET]. Exceeding either limit makes the project ineligible for global judging - Paris kit [VERIFIED 3rd party, 2025].
- The video may be spoken in another language but "MUST include English language subtitles" - project-submission-guide [SNIPPET].
- "You will not be able to upload your video or slides to the NASA Space Apps website", so host it publicly and link it - project-submission-guide [SNIPPET].
- The final project link must be public, with no password, permission request or registration: a website, deployed app or repo - Paris kit [VERIFIED 3rd party, 2025].
- Suggested 30 s video beats: team (0:00), challenge and inspiration (0:02), title and tagline (0:05), how it works + data (0:10), impact (0:20-0:30) - Paris kit [VERIFIED 3rd party].

## 4. JUDGING CRITERIA
- Five criteria: **Impact (formerly "Influence"), Creativity, Validity, Relevance, Presentation**. Each is scored "on a scale of 1-to-5", and teams are told to pay "equal attention to the final criterion: presentation" - judging-awards-guide [SNIPPET]. **No weights are published, so treat them as equal** [INFERRED]. The criteria name in 2026 is "Impact" (search summary of submission guide + https://mindshipglobal.com/opportunities/nasa-space-apps-challenge-2026/ [SNIPPET]); the 2024 guide used "Influence" (https://www.scribd.com/document/876777314/Judging-Awards-Guideline [SNIPPET]).
- Definitions (exact 2026 wording NOT obtained; paraphrases/older wording):
  - Impact: "potential of the proposed solution to address meaningful challenges and benefit people, science or society" (mindshipglobal 2026, 3rd party [SNIPPET]). 2024: "the quality and scale of a project's impact" (Scribd 2024 [SNIPPET]).
  - Creativity: how innovative the approach is; is it new or only an incremental improvement (http://2018.spaceappschallenge.org/locations/grodno/ [SNIPPET, 2018]).
  - Validity: is it scientifically sound, will it do what it sets out to do, can it work in the real world (https://2021.spaceappschallenge.org/locations/rome/event/ [SNIPPET, 2021]).
  - Relevance: does the project respond to the challenge it was submitted to (2018 Grodno [SNIPPET]).
  - Presentation: how well the team communicated the project (2018 Grodno [SNIPPET]).

Only Space Apps branding is allowed - https://assets.spaceappschallenge.org/media/documents/Space_Apps_Brand_Guide_VUGpVJm.pdf and https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/navigator-terms-conditions/ [SNIPPET].

## 8. UNIVERSAL EVENT vs LOCAL EVENT
- Universal Event = virtual. Universal Event Judges pick which projects advance to Global Judging. Its share is "proportional to the number of projects that advance ... from Local Events, usually around one Global Nominee per ten projects submitted", and the selection rate is "comparable across both" - judging-awards-guide + participant-faqs [SNIPPET].
- Local events: Local Leads/Judges choose the nominees and may give local awards. **No fixed per-event nominee cap was found**; the ~1-in-10 ratio is the best guide [INFERRED].
- Deadline basis differs: the Universal Event follows the Team Owner's residence time (2025 [SNIPPET]); a local event follows that event's local time [INFERRED].
