## Rules that can disqualify you
- Must answer an official challenge ("Create Your Own Challenge" can't win global awards).
- Must use NASA data. Teams are 1–6 people, all registered at the same event. Everything in English.
- Demo: a video of 30 seconds or less (English subtitles) or up to 7 slides, at a public link with no login.
- References must list every external resource. Declare all AI use and watermark AI images/video. No NASA logos.
- No copyrighted music.
- There is a review for prohibited work with China.
- Judging criteria: Impact (renamed from "Influence"), Creativity, Validity, Relevance, Presentation. Each scored 1–5; treat them as equal weight.

## What wins (01)
1. **Win the room first.** Only 1–3 projects per local event advance [V, 2022 guide]. 2025 had 45 finalists for 10 awards.
2. **Pick the award on purpose.** Winners match their award's written definition. One challenge often produced 2–3 winners in different awards [V, NASA.gov lists].
3. **Obvious build plus one meaningful twist.** Example: SkyShield 2024 built the standard orrery but added meteoroid risk to satellites. Generic exoplanet-AI projects and paper chatbots stalled at Cincinnati 2025 [V].
4. **Pitch in one sentence, naming a user and a payoff.** Every winner's NASA summary fits that shape.
5. **Validity** clearly matters for the Science, Data and Technology awards. For the other awards, clarity and fit with the audience matter more [I].

Hypotheses from the brief:
- (1) tightly scoped, visible NASA data: supported.
- (2) understandable in 30 seconds: supported.
- (3) pick a target award: supported.
- (4) validity beats pretty dashboards: partly, mainly for the technical awards.
- (5) crowded builds rarely win: supported, but crowded challenges still produce winners when a team adds a strong twist.

## Sonification track record (01)
- Sonification won twice: SongSAT, 2018, Most Inspirational; Arcobaleno, 2023, Global Connection [V].
- **Both framed the sound as access for blind and low-vision people.**
- The naive mapping (scan the image left to right; brightness → volume, height → pitch) got SPACETUNE 2022 only to Nominee [V].
- 2023's sound challenge had at least 10 public repos and produced 1 winner [V].

## Preliminary read on Jukebox
**Not a verdict yet** (that's Phase 2). Crowded and easy, so the default build loses.

The only credible path:
- Sound generated from the actual EIC frames.
- An accessibility framing.
- A measured blind or low-vision listening test (for example, "listeners identified X by ear 8/10 times").
- Target Global Connection or Art & Technology.

Without those, PIVOT.
