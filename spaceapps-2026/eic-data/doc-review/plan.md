- Cross-origin lock: a browser demo that reads NASA-hosted images, videos or JSON directly will fail (fetch blocked, canvas tainted). Discovered late, this breaks the demo; plan a mirror or an offline pre-processing step from the start.
- Data weight and format: EXR and TIF frames, folders of 2-707 GB, HEVC and ProRes movies. Bulk download is impossible in the time left; use small h264 or WebM movies, PNG/JPG stills, or single frames.
