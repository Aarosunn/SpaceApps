With the official counts and the catalog's own criteria (under-served + feasible + award fit), the Jukebox moves from rank 6 to rank 1 (inference). RESULT 3 (shared work / fallback): no other challenge uses EIC frames or sound as its input. The realistic same-day fallbacks, in order, are Be an Earth System Trend Detective (smallest official scope: one time series plus one significance test), Harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS Hot Spots (third fewest teams; the EIC gallery itself contains VIIRS active-fire pages, so a fire-themed Jukebox shares domain work), and Abandoned but Not Forgotten (audio/podcast format is explicitly allowed, but it is very crowded). Dancing with the SARs and Planet X and SPHEREx are poor pivots today because of data difficulty; SPHEREx is also the second most chosen challenge at the Chicago event (10 of 87 teams).
