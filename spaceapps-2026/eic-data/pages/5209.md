## Quick answer

- What it is: Small globe split into 8 latitude bands plus a bar chart: each band's annual temperature anomaly (vs 1951-1980) as a coloured box with ghost bars for earlier years, 1880 to 2023.
- Kind: chart-or-graph-animation
- Data source: GISTEMP v4 zonal annual anomalies (baseline 1951-1980) (?)
- Period covered: 1880 to 2023, one year at a time (label 1880 at t=0, 1931 at 10 s, 1977 at 19 s, 2023 at 29 s)
- Time step of the underlying data: annual (one value per 8 latitude zones per year)

- Suggested mapping: Each latitude band = one voice (north high pitch, south low); box x position (anomaly in degC) = pitch bend or detune; year = playback step (about 5 years per second as in the movie).

## Gotchas

- Year step is annual with smooth in-betweens; do not treat frames as months or days.
- Frame set is EXR (3840x2160, 3.9 GB), page says 901 items but listing has 900.
- Title says 1880-2023; newer 5451 exists for 2024 (not in this cluster).
- Ghost bars from earlier years are grey strips behind the coloured box: needs a colour filter to find the current box.
- Globe band colours repeat the same values as the boxes (redundant).
