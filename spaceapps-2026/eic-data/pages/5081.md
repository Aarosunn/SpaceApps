## Quick answer

- What it is: Six yearly (2015 to 2020) national CO2 budgets for 100+ countries drawn as extruded country polygons on a Robinson-like flat world map; 16 movies/stills in light and dark versions, no continuous time axis.
- Kind: data-animation
- Data source: Pilot top-down CO2 Budget constrained by the v10 OCO-2 MIP Version 1 (DOI 10.48588/npf6-sw92); Byrne et al. 2023 ESSD; scientist Brendan Byrne (JPL) (mixed)
- Period covered: Six calendar years 2015 to 2020 (big year text '2015', '2016', '2018' seen in frames; '2020' in the print still; '2015-2020' in the mean still). 2017 and 2019 not directly seen.
- Time step of the underlying data: Yearly national mean, units PgCO2 per year = billion metric tons CO2 per year. No sub-annual data.
- **One frame equals**: Not a data step. Each year holds ~64.8 frames (389 frames / 6) in the vertical movie or 60 frames (360 / 6) in the 1920x1080 movies, with probable cross-fades. One frame is an animation frame, not a time sample.
- **One frame = one day?**: not-applicable
- Where a program gets a frame's date: 2015 (first year); last year 2020
- Date label seen on screen: Yes: large light text '2015' / '2016' / '2018' top-left in the 1080x1920 movie; '2020' bottom-left in the 1920x1080 variant; '2015-2020' in the mean still. Title 'National Carbon Dioxide Budgets'; panel titles 'Net Surface Emissions & Removals of Carbon Dioxide', 'Fossil Fuel Emissions', 'Terrestrial Carbon Stock Change'. 'No Data' grey swatch; colorbar 'CO2 Emissions (PgCO2 per year)' with ticks -2.5, 0, 3 and 'Billion metric tons of CO2 per year'. 1920x1080 variant adds '1 PgCO2 per year = 1 Billion metric tons of CO2 per year'. No NASA logo seen.
- Update behaviour: Static. Released 2023-03-07; page record updated 2024-02-21. Movie Last-Modified 2023-03-06/07; frame files all Mar 6, 2023; stills re-exported 2023-03-15. No rolling window.
- Status on 2026-11-14: static
- View / camera: Fixed camera, flat Robinson-style oval world map; countries extruded in 3D by value (red pops out, green sinks), light graticule, grey 'No Data' for non-covered land and Antarctica. Three stacked maps (net, fossil, terrestrial) in the vertical movie; one map per movie in the 1920x1080 versions. Ocean grey (Light) or darker (Dark). Extrusion shading leaves hatched side walls on Alaska/China/US.
- On-screen clutter: Moderate: title, year, colorbar, No Data swatch, panel titles. No clean alpha/data layer; no frame set at 1920x1080 (only the vertical set of 358 PNGs). White-background 4000x3000 stills (PNG and EXR) exist.
- Clean variant: Partly: Light and Dark versions, per-variable movies, 4000x3000 white stills. One flat colour per country polygon so a country lookup from a still is feasible. No version without year or colorbar.
- What the colours mean: Diverging scale: dark green = net removal (down to -2.5 PgCO2/yr), pale near 0, brown to dark red = net emission (up to +3 PgCO2/yr, saturating). Same bar for all three panels, per country. Grey = no data. Extrusion height is redundant with value.
- Fit as a sonification source: **good** — Country polygons with a fixed diverging colorbar and big year label let a cursor read a value (colour -> PgCO2/yr) with no rotation or camera motion. Only 6 years and ~100 countries so it is sparse but precise; the net/fossil/terrestrial triplet gives three voices per country.
- Suggested mapping: Cursor over a country: look up its colour on the colorbar (-2.5..+3) -> pitch (green low, red high), volume = |value|, chord change when the sign flips at 0; step 2015 to 2020 as six notes.

## Frame → time mapping

Date comes ONLY from the big year number burned into the picture (needs OCR or a hard-coded lookup). Inferred formula: year = 2015 + floor(frame / (389/6)) for the 1080x1920 movie (checked: frame 0 -> 2015; frame ~128 at t=4.27 s -> 2016, floor(128/64.8)=1; frame ~256 at t=8.53 s -> 2018, floor(256/64.8)=3). For 360-frame movies floor(frame/60) is UNVERIFIED. Best practice: sample at 0.25 s steps and hard-code frame ranges.

## Gotchas

- Year exists only as burned-in text; ~65 frames per year with probable cross-fades, so in-between frames blend two years.
- Extruded polygons are shaded; side-wall pixels are darker than the colorbar value, so sample the top-face centre.
- Colorbar saturates at +3 (US, China clipped); no value above ~3 can be read.
- Light vs Dark use different ocean colour; grey No Data also covers oceans and Antarctica.
- Frame set has 358 files (001..358) while the video probes ~389 frames and the page says 359; the 1920x1080 movies are 12.00 s / 360 frames.
- Per-country values are not on the page; they are in the ESSD paper and the DOI dataset.
- Hyperwall .hwshow entries for per-variable movies show HTTP 0 (missing).

##### Media group 312849 | widget: Basic text with HTML | title: (none)
Description: This data visualization shows the emissions (sources) and removals (sinks) of carbon dioxide (CO₂) for more than 100 countries around the world. These estimates are informed by observations of atmospheric CO₂ observed by a network of ground-based sites and NASA’s Orbiting Carbon Observatory 2 (OCO-2), which has mapped  CO₂ around the globe since 2014.

The net emissions and removals are the combined impact of fossil fuel emissions and land carbon stock changes. These terrestrial carbon stock changes indicate changes in the amount of carbon stored in organic matter on land, which is impacted by land use and land use change, including emissions from deforestation and removals from improved land stewardship and reforestation.

The visualization shows that fossil fuel emissions drive net emissions of CO₂ to the atmosphere, which are in turn driving climate change. Terrestrial carbon stock changes are smaller than the fossil fuel emissions and regionally varying.  In general, terrestrial carbon stocks are increasing across the northern extratropics but are more variable in the tropics, where deforestation is more extensive.
