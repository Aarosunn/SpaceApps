- Kind: chart-or-graph-animation
- Data source: NASA OCO-2 (Reanalysis, global) and NOAA Mauna Loa Observatory monthly CO2 (Keeling Curve); no datasets credited on page (?)
- Period covered: Sweep phase: Jan 2015 to Mar 2023 (labels seen: Jan 2015 at t=0, Aug 2016 at 2 s, Nov 2019 at 6 s, Jul 2021 at 8 s, Mar 2023 at 10 s). Zoom-out phase (about 10 s to 30 s): x axis widens to 2008-2023 (t=19 s) and 1960-2023 (t=29 s); time label stays Mar 2023.

## Gotchas

- Only the first ~10 s of 30 s advance in time; the rest is an x-axis zoom-out frozen at Mar 2023.
- Right-hand label changes from 'Mauna Loa Observatory' to 'Maunakea Observatories' between t=8 s and t=10 s.
- OCO-2 is a global reanalysis, Mauna Loa is one site; readouts differ by 1 to 3 ppm.
- Page says 901 frames for the EXR set; listing has 900 (00001 to 00900).
- File numbering starts at 00001, not 0.
- x range differs between t=19 s (2008-2023) and t=29 s (1960-2023), so pixel x does not map to a fixed date across the movie.
