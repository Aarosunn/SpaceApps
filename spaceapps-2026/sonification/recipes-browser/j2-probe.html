Move the probe over the map to hear the value under it. A higher note means a higher value, and the sound moves left and right with the probe. Keyboard: put the focus on the map, then use the arrow keys (hold Shift for bigger steps), Home and End for the left and right edge, Page Up and Page Down for the top and bottom, Space to repeat the reading, and R for the reference tones.
