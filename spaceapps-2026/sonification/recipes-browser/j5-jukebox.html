    How to listen

      The sound legend plays five sounds, 1.2 seconds apart, in this order:

      1. A low lead note: the lowest level in this clip.
      2. A high lead note: the highest level in this clip.
      3. The reference blip: the middle of the range.
      4. A drum on the left: an event in the west.
      5. A drum on the right: an event in the east.
