Where each note comes from

NASA SVS 3912, sea surface temperature. Press play. Four notes are played every second.

Loading the note list.

  • White frame = pitch source. Each note's pitch comes from the average temperature of every ocean cell in view, not from one spot. It flashes on each note.
  • Yellow column = left/right source. The warmest of the eight columns decides which ear the note leans towards.
  • Numbers are the temperature (°C) read from the pixels of each of the 4 x 8 cells. Cells showing a dash are land.
